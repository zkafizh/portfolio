Gas Leak Alert, Software Developer
Gas Leak Alert is an affordable IoT-based safety system designed to detect LPG gas leaks in residential areas. The system uses an MQ-6 gas sensor connected to an ESP32 microcontroller to continuously monitor gas levels. When a dangerous gas concentration is detected, the system activates a local buzzer and LED warning while simultaneously sending a real-time notification to the user's smartphone through a mobile application. This project aims to provide an accessible early-warning solution that helps residents respond quickly to gas leaks, even when they are away from home.
